# People: experts, influencers, guest speakers

Goal: a table of people who could enrich the course on the topic as guest speakers,
interview partners, or whose public output (talks, blogs, packages, papers) students should
know. Three location tiers: **Wien**, **Österreich**, **Global**. Aim for a balanced mix of
academics, practitioners (industry, consulting, agencies, start-ups) and communicators
(bloggers, YouTubers, podcast hosts, LinkedIn voices).

## Where to look

- Conference programmes and speaker lists: EMAC, AMA, ISMS Marketing Science, ICIS, PyData
  Vienna, useR!/posit::conf, EuroPython, Data Natives, WeAreDevelopers (Vienna), Vienna Data
  Science Group meetups, Marketing Natives, ÖMG/ÖWA events, DMVÖ, Austrian Marketing Day
- Austrian institutions: WU Wien institutes (Marketing, Statistics, Data Science), Uni Wien,
  TU Wien (data science), JKU Linz, Uni Graz, MODUL, FH Wien der WKW, FH Technikum, FH St.
  Pölten, Complexity Science Hub, AIT, Statistik Austria, Austrian National Bank research
- Austrian industry: analytics teams and agencies in Vienna (e.g. at telcos, retailers,
  banks, insurers, media houses, adtech/martech firms, consultancies), Austrian start-ups
  on the topic
- Global: package authors (GitHub, CRAN, PyPI maintainers), authors of the books and
  articles the other agents found, hosts of relevant podcasts and YouTube channels,
  LinkedIn Top Voices and Substack authors on the topic, methods bloggers
- Google Scholar profiles and OpenAlex author pages for citation context; LinkedIn, personal
  sites, X/Bluesky/Mastodon for current roles and talk activity; YouTube/podcast appearances
  as evidence they can present

## What to record (professional information only)

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| Titel / Name | full name as used professionally |
| Autor / Quelle / Firma | current affiliation and role; location tag **Wien**, **Österreich** or **Global**; type: academic / practitioner / communicator |
| Link | one main public professional profile (personal website, LinkedIn public profile, Google Scholar, GitHub, institution page) |
| Notiz | why relevant to the topic: key publications, packages, talks, products, follower reach; language(s) they present in |
| Verwendung im Kurs | guest-speaker fit (lecture, panel, Q&A, recorded interview), practical angle, realistic format (on site in Vienna, remote), any known speaking history |
| Status | Link geprüft / in Suchergebnis bestätigt / Link ungeprüft |

Only use information from public professional profiles and publications. Do not record
private contact data (personal e-mail, phone, home address), personal characteristics, or
anything from non-professional social media. Where an institutional e-mail is on an
official page, "contact via institute page" is enough; the instructor can look it up.

## Quality

A good row says what the person has actually done on the topic (a package, a talk, a book
chapter, a case) and why a business student would learn from them. Avoid generic "data
science influencer" rows without topic link. 10 to 25 well-chosen people beat 60 names.
